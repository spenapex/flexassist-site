Flex Assist Support

A workout log with AI help. Free to use, no account needed.

Need help? Email flexassist.support@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. You can also tap Profile > Report a Problem in the app, which adds this for you.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Flex Assist has no sign-up and no login. Your data lives on your iPhone.

How do I import an exercise from TikTok or Instagram?

Open Library > + > Import from TikTok / Reel (or tap Import Reel in a workout), paste the link and tap Generate Your Gains. Flex Assist reads the public caption and Claude turns it into an exercise with steps. It never downloads the video.

The import says it can't read the caption

TikTok and Instagram sometimes block apps from reading captions. Open the post, copy its caption, paste it into the box that appears and tap Generate Your Gains again. You can also use Library > + > Describe to Claude and type what the exercise is.

How do I add an exercise by chatting with Claude?

Go to Library > + > Describe to Claude, describe the exercise in your own words, review or edit the draft and tap Save to Library.

What gets sent to AI, and can I turn it off?

Only what you give an AI feature: a link or caption, your chat messages, and exercise names, muscles and machine brands. It is sent through the Flex Assist server to Anthropic and Google. Your workouts, weights, measurements and photos are never sent. Turn AI off any time in Settings > AI & Privacy; everything else keeps working. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Profile > Settings > Delete All My Data. This removes all workouts, routines, photos and settings from your iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.

How do I export my workouts?

Profile > Settings > Your Data > Export saves every finished workout as a CSV file you can open in Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets.

Why did my AI request fail or say "too many requests"?

AI features need an internet connection, and there is a limit on how fast they can be used. Wait a minute and try again. If it keeps failing, send a report from the app.

Can I switch between pounds and kilograms?

Yes, in Settings > Units. Your logged weights are kept exactly as entered; only how they are shown changes.

What does "Previous" show when I change a machine brand?

It shows your last session of that exercise on the same brand. If you haven't used that brand yet, it shows your numbers from another brand and tells you so.

Is the recovery map medical advice?

No. It is an estimate from the sets you logged. Flex Assist is a training log, not a medical tool. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new program, and stop if something hurts.

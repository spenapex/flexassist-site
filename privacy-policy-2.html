Flex Assist Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Developer: Spencer Thom

The short version. Flex Assist has no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your workouts, measurements and photos stay on your iPhone. The only things that leave your phone are what you give an AI feature (with your permission) and any feedback you choose to send.

1. What stays on your device

Flex Assist stores the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • Workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps and notes
  • Routines, custom exercises and machine brands you add
  • Body measurements and progress photos, if you add them
  • Your profile name and photo, if you add them
  • Settings such as units, theme, rest timer and favorites

We do not receive, copy or store any of this. If you use iCloud or computer backups, your device may include the app's data in those backups according to your device settings. You can delete everything at any time in Settings > Delete All My Data, or by deleting the app.

2. AI features and what is sent

Flex Assist includes optional AI features. Before any of them runs for the first time, the app asks for your permission. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > AI & Privacy, and the rest of the app keeps working.

FeatureWhat is sentWho processes it

Never sent to an AI service: your workout history, sets, weights, body measurements, progress photos, profile, or the photos you add to exercises.

The Flex Assist server is a small relay that adds the AI providers' access keys and builds the request. It does not have a database and does not keep your prompts or results. Cloudflare may keep standard operational logs (such as request time and error messages) for a limited time. Anthropic and Google handle the requests they receive under their own terms and privacy policies, which may include limited retention for safety and abuse monitoring. See:

  • Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare Privacy Policy

3. TikTok and Instagram links

When you paste a link, Flex Assist reads only the public caption text of that post so it can build an exercise. It does not download, save, copy or play any video, audio or images from TikTok or Instagram. The link itself is saved on your device next to the exercise so you can open the original post later. To read the caption, your phone or the Flex Assist server contacts TikTok or Instagram, and those services can see that request (including the network address it came from) under their own privacy policies. Flex Assist is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok or Instagram.

4. Exercise photos

Photos for the built-in exercise library are loaded from a public exercise dataset hosted on GitHub. When they load, GitHub can see your network address, as with any website. Photos you add yourself stay on your device.

5. Feedback and bug reports

If you send feedback or report a problem from within the app, we receive the message you write. We also receive your email address if you choose to enter one, and basic device information if you leave that switch on (app version, iOS version, device model and the number of exercises loaded). It is delivered to the developer by email through an email service (Resend) and used only to understand and answer your report. Emails are kept as long as needed to respond and fix the problem, and you can ask us to delete them (section 9).

6. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, phone number or location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • We do not read your Health data or connect to Apple Health.

7. Permissions

  • Photos: only the pictures you pick yourself, through the system photo picker. The app cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Notifications: optional, used only to tell you when a rest timer ends.

8. Children

Flex Assist is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your choices and rights

You can use the app without AI features, delete all on-device data in Settings, or export your workouts as a CSV file. Because we do not hold your workout data or an account for you, there is nothing else to retrieve or erase on our side except any feedback email you sent. To ask us to delete a feedback email, or if you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, contact us at the address below and we will respond.

10. Health notice

Flex Assist is a training log and planning tool. Recovery estimates, progress suggestions and AI-generated exercises are general information, not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new exercise program, and stop if something hurts.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for changes that affect what is sent from your device, ask for your permission again in the app.
